My story

I am Gustian Rabi Abdul Jabar, a graduate of the University of Indonesia. I am a game programmer and developer — if you liked my work (I like you already), let's get in contact.

In my childhood I used to build the programs I imagined. I really enjoyed games like Counter-Strike, Final Fantasy, and 2D and 3D puzzle games. At that time I decided I would make truly fantastic games in the future — that is why I became a game and app developer.

My first project was a card game like poker. I designed many of the card images and built the gameplay system in Unity. While developing it I realised how powerful Unity is and wanted to contribute to its community, so I made some Unity assets myself. I also like 3D modeling: at university I designed and modeled the campus building in 3ds Max, learning colour, line and shape as well as modeling, texturing, lighting and rendering.

I built a 3D game called With Puppy — a play-to-earn game that encourages players to exercise. Players walk with a virtual puppy or cat along a predefined route; the game tracks their steps and, based on distance walked, rewards in-game currency called diamonds. It includes trading, renting and reward systems. I also optimized the game for Android and iOS and collaborated with designers on 3D assets to ensure smooth gameplay.
